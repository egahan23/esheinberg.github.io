## Dissertation

### Determinants and health impacts of nutrition incentive program use

A mixed-methods study of how SNAP participants in Massachusetts and Indiana use nutrition incentives over time.

- **Massachusetts:** SNAP and Healthy Incentives Program (HIP) administrative transaction data, used to understand who uses incentives and how their use changes over time.
- **Indiana:** Longitudinal transaction data, program evaluation surveys, and qualitative interviews with Fresh Bucks participants, used to understand how personal, social, and environmental contexts shape incentive use.

Supported by an American Heart Association Pre-Doctoral Fellowship and a Rose Service-Learning Fellowship. Advisor: Cindy W. Leung.

## COVID-19 SNAP Waivers

How COVID-19 waivers affected SNAP participants' food security and health, and how state SNAP agencies implemented and unwound them. This team project draws on original surveys, qualitative interviews, and secondary data.

## SNAP Restriction Waivers

An evaluation of state waivers that remove sugary drinks and candy from SNAP eligibility, examining effects on diet quality and stigma.
